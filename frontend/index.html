Environmental Engineering Consultancy

With expertise across the complete water-wastewater cycle, Waves Envirotech delivers end-to-end engineering solutions — from concept to commissioning — across 7 major verticals.

Water Treatment

Conventional & advanced WTPs from 1 MLD to 550 MLD. Inclined Plate Settlers, Lamella Clarifiers, Constant Rate Filtration.

WTP

Sewage Treatment

ASP, A2O, SBR, MBBR, IFAS, MBR & Phytorid-based STPs for municipal, smart city and residential applications.

STP

Effluent Treatment

Custom ETPs for textile, pharma, chemical, petrochemical & food industries with physico-chemical & biological processes.

ETP

Desalination

Sea & brackish water desalination using SWRO, BWRO, NF & hybrid membrane technologies for coastal utilities & industries.

DESAL

Pre-bid Consultancy

Expert pre-bid consultancy, feasibility studies, Detailed Project Reports & technical specifications for government clients.

DPR

Membrane Technologies

MF, UF, NF & RO systems for water recycling, Zero Liquid Discharge, tertiary treatment & industrial effluent recovery.

MBR / RO

Zero Liquid Discharge

Complete ZLD solutions for industries requiring 100% wastewater recycling — evaporators, crystallizers & brine management.
