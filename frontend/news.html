Waves Envirotech Completes Pre-bid Consultancy for Major Delhi STP Projects

Waves Envirotech Pvt. Ltd. has successfully completed its pre-bid consultancy assignment for multiple Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) under Delhi Jal Board (DJB). The consultancy scope included preparation of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), techno-economic feasibility studies, and complete tender documentation for four STPs with a combined capacity of over 300 MLD. The projects are to be funded under JNNURM and will significantly boost Delhi's sewage treatment infrastructure.
