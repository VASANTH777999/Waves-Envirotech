Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and how we work with clients.

Waves Envirotech handles the full spectrum of water treatment projects — from small-scale rural water supply schemes to large-capacity municipal Water Treatment Plants (WTPs) up to several hundred MLD. Our expertise includes conventional treatment systems, advanced filtration, membrane-based systems (UF/RO/NF), desalination, and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems for industries.
Yes. Waves Envirotech has successfully delivered projects in multiple countries across South Asia and Southeast Asia, including Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Singapore. We have worked with international funding agencies including the Asian Development Bank (ADB), Agence Française de Développement (AFD), KfW (German Development Bank), and the World Bank on internationally funded projects.
Pre-bid consultancy involves preparing detailed project reports (DPRs), techno-economic feasibility studies, tender documents, bid evaluation reports, and technical specifications for water infrastructure projects — typically before the construction tender is issued. This ensures that the implementing agency has robust documentation, realistic cost estimates, and technically sound designs before inviting contractors. It reduces project risks and delays significantly.
The timeline varies based on project complexity and scope. A DPR or feasibility study typically takes 2–4 months. Detailed engineering design for a mid-size WTP or STP (10–50 MLD) usually requires 3–6 months. Full pre-bid consultancy including tender documentation can take 4–8 months. We provide a precise project schedule during the initial scoping stage for all engagements.

Beyond design and construction supervision, Waves Envirotech offers O&M advisory services including preparation of O&M manuals, performance audit of existing treatment plants, process optimization studies, troubleshooting support, and capacity building training programs for plant operators and municipal staff. We help clients maximize the operational efficiency and longevity of their water infrastructure investments.
