Membranes

Advanced Membrane Technologies

Membrane technologies represent the cutting edge of water and wastewater treatment. Waves Envirotech's leadership has received specialized international training at NORIT Technologies, Netherlands (UF membranes) and has extensive hands-on experience designing MF, UF, NF, and RO systems for municipal, industrial, and commercial applications.

MF — Microfiltration

0.1–0.5 μm pore size. Removes suspended solids, bacteria, and large colloids. Pre-treatment for RO/NF systems.

UF — Ultrafiltration

0.01–0.1 μm. Removes viruses, colloids, and macromolecules. Trained at NORIT Technologies, Netherlands.

NF — Nanofiltration

1–10 nm. Removes divalent ions, color, and organic matter. Intermediate between UF and RO.

RO — Reverse Osmosis

<1 nm. Removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, and most organics. Core of desalination and ZLD systems.

  • MBR (Membrane Bioreactor) for high-quality STP effluent
  • UF as pre-treatment to RO for stable, reliable desalination
  • Industrial effluent recycling using UF+RO (Raymonds, CPCL)
  • 60 MGD WTP with MF/UF (fully automated), PUB Singapore
