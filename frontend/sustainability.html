Key Initiatives

Our Sustainability Initiatives

ZLD Technology

Zero Liquid Discharge — Eliminating Wastewater

Waves Envirotech is a pioneer in Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) technology in India. Our ZLD systems ensure that industrial effluents are treated and recycled to the point where there is absolutely no liquid waste discharged into the environment.

We have successfully implemented ZLD solutions for major industries including TATA Steel, Reliance Industries and CPCL, helping them achieve full regulatory compliance and contribute to water conservation.

100% Water Recovery Zero Environmental Discharge
Renewable Energy

Solar-Powered Water Treatment Plants

We integrate solar energy systems with water treatment plants to significantly reduce carbon footprints and operating costs. Our solar-augmented WTP designs can reduce energy consumption by up to 60% compared to conventional plants.

By combining renewable energy with advanced treatment technologies, we help municipalities and industries achieve both environmental and economic sustainability targets.

Up to 60% Energy Savings Carbon Footprint Reduction
Water Reuse

Treated Water Reuse & Recycling Programmes

We design tertiary treatment and water recycling systems that enable treated effluent to be reused for irrigation, industrial cooling, groundwater recharge and non-potable municipal applications — reducing freshwater demand significantly.

Our reuse programmes help bridge the water demand-supply gap in water-stressed regions, contributing directly to national water security goals.

Reduce Freshwater Demand Groundwater Recharge
Green Tech

Eco-Friendly Technologies We Deploy

Our technology portfolio focuses on minimising environmental impact while maximising treatment efficiency and sustainability.

Membrane Bio-Reactor (MBR)

High-quality effluent suitable for direct reuse, compact footprint, minimal sludge generation.

Sequential Batch Reactor (SBR)

Energy-efficient biological treatment with no moving parts, ideal for variable flows.

Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR)

High biomass density, compact design, energy savings of up to 40% vs conventional processes.

UV Disinfection

Chemical-free disinfection eliminating chlorination by-products and harmful DBPs.

Reverse Osmosis (RO)

Energy-efficient membranes for desalination and water purification with full water recovery.

Biogas Recovery

Methane capture from sludge digestion — converts waste to renewable energy on-site.

Energy Recovery Devices

Pressure exchangers and turbines in desalination plants reduce energy by 50-60%.

Natural Treatment Systems

Constructed wetlands, reed beds and soil aquifer treatment for decentralised applications.

Commitments

Our Environmental Commitments

Beyond certifications, we make concrete commitments that guide how we work every day:

Water Conservation First

All designs prioritise water reuse and recycling, minimising fresh water extraction from natural sources.

Energy-Efficient Design

We set energy reduction targets for every project and achieve them through smart engineering and technology selection.

Minimise Sludge Generation

Advanced treatment processes are selected to minimise sludge, and where generated, it is converted to useful biogas or compost.

Carbon Footprint Reduction

We measure, report, and continuously reduce the carbon footprint of all projects we design and manage.

Stakeholder Education

We actively train client personnel, operators and communities in sustainable water management practices.
