Who We Are

A Legacy of Environmental Engineering

Waves Envirotech Pvt. Ltd. is a premier environmental engineering and consultancy company headquartered in Hyderabad, India. Founded with a mission to deliver world-class water and wastewater engineering solutions, we have grown into a nationally recognized and internationally respected organization with a presence across India and multiple countries.

Our core strength lies in the seamless integration of deep technical expertise, cutting-edge technology, and a thorough understanding of regulatory and institutional frameworks. We specialize in conceptualization, design, detailed engineering, pre-bid consultancy, and DPR preparation for Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), Desalination Plants, Tertiary Treatment Plants, and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems.

  • ISO-certified quality management processes
  • Trusted by 30+ major municipal bodies and government agencies
  • International project experience across 8+ countries
  • 150+ projects completed across municipal, industrial, and institutional sectors
  • Expertise in 15+ advanced treatment technologies
  • Leadership team trained internationally in Netherlands, Austria & UK

Our Journey

Building a Legacy Over Decades

Waves Envirotech's journey is one of continuous growth, technical mastery, and expanding impact — from early domestic projects to landmark international assignments.

Early Years

Foundation & Early Projects

Established with a vision to address India's growing water and wastewater infrastructure challenges. Initiated with municipal STP and WTP design and consultancy projects across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Growth Phase

National Expansion

Expanded consultancy footprint across multiple Indian states. Executed landmark projects for HMWSSB (410 MLD WTP), GVMC Vizag, and multiple ULBs under JNNURM funding. Established reputation as a leading pre-bid consultancy firm.

International Milestones

Global Recognition

Won international assignments — 400 MLD STP Changi Singapore, 85 MLD WTP Sri Lanka (ADB/AFD). International training in Netherlands and Austria.

Today

28+ Years of Excellence

Over 150 projects completed. Trusted partner for Delhi Jal Board, BWSSB, AMC, GNIDA, KMC, KWA, and dozens of institutions. Recognized leader in SBR, MBBR, MBR, ZLD, and advanced membrane technologies.
